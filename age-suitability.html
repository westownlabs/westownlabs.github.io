5 Second Animal

5 Second Animal is a multiplayer party drawing game designed for players aged 13 and older.

Content

The app contains:

  • User-generated drawings — players draw prompts freehand within a time limit. Drawings are visible to other players in the same game room.
  • User-generated text — players can create custom prompts and choose display names. These are visible to other players in the same room.
  • Advertising — the app displays rewarded interstitial ads between game rounds via Google AdMob.

What the App Does Not Contain

  • No violent, sexual, or graphic content is provided by the app itself
  • No in-app purchases or loot boxes
  • No social media integration or public profiles
  • No location tracking
  • No access to contacts, camera, or microphone

Moderation

Games take place in private rooms created by players and shared via invite links or QR codes. There is no public matchmaking or open chat. Room owners can remove or ban players from their rooms.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
